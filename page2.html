Pandas Are Not Like the Others

As you can see above Pandas are a lot smaller than their brothers. However, they can reach up to 6 feet tall .

Did you know that unlike other bears pandas do not hibernate? Instead of hibernating they move to a higher elevation.

Pandas are also the only ones that primarily eat bamboo. 99% of their diet consists of bamboo, the other 1% can be either grass or other insects.

Pandas are the only bears with pseudothumbs, which helps with their hearty diet of bamboo. Pandas ranked 5th in their bite force with other carnivores following behind tigers, grizzly bears, polar bears, and lions. Let's not make them angry...
